Interactive Assessment Ecosystem Readiness

5-Question Replenishment Posture Evaluator

Score your ecosystem across the 5 core replenishment dimensions to determine your phase and next steps.

1

Ratio — What is your replenishment ratio today?

Measures the ratio of collected value to deployed maintenance budget and how gaps are subsidized.

2

Anchors — Does collection attach to fork-resistant anchors?

Tests whether avoidance requires giving up valuable services, state, or relationships.

3

Bundles — Is optional payment attached to tangible benefits?

Evaluates whether payments purchase products payers independently value ("Sold, not taxed").

4

Mandate — Are collection rails chartered and audited?

Ensures collection requires the same authorization and audit discipline as spending.

5

Runway — Can baseline maintenance survive a full bear market?

Tests whether non-inflationary income and liquid buffers cover baseline costs during market downturns.
