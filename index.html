Decentralized Open Source Program Office (dOSPO)

WHO: Policy Engine, Institutional Authority & Bounded Mandate

A dOSPO operates as a neutral, on-chain or community-governed coordination layer bridging governance bodies with maintainers and enterprise consumers.

  • 📜 Charter & Authority: Time-limited, evidence-based mandate from community referenda.
  • 🛡️ Neutrality Guarantee: Complete independence from single commercial sponsors.
  • 🔄 Operator Replaceability: Governance can replace the operating team without disrupting core software.

Open Maintenance Framework (OMF)

HOW SPEND: Programmatic Deployment & Contributor Retainers

OMF standardizes how sustainability capital is allocated to maintainers, core tooling, and security dependencies.

  • ⏳ Maintainer Retainers: 12-month streamed compensation for core maintainers.
  • 🗺️ Dependency Stewardship: Systematic identification and funding of critical upstream tools.
  • 🪜 Contributor Pathways: Structured grant ladders and onboarding for new contributors.

Open Replenishment Framework (ORF)

HOW COLLECT: Value-Aligned Inflows & Sustained Enterprise Mechanics

ORF grounds 20+ non-inflationary collection mechanisms that route commercial value back into ecosystem maintenance funds.

  • 🤝 Enterprise SLAs: Commercial maintenance service agreements and patch windows.
  • 🛡️ Sustains-the-Commons Badges: Registry badges and state verification for commercial integrations.
  • 📈 IPS Endowments: Treasury yield sleeves generating baseline maintenance income.
