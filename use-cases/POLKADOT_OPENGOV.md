## 1. Executive Summary & Ecosystem Overview

The **Polkadot ecosystem** provides two vital Web3 reference implementations:
1. **Polkadot Technical Fellowship**: A transparent, rank-based maintainer progression ladder (Ranks 0 through 9) with automated, tenure-weighted stipends.
2. **Polkadot Community Foundation (PCF)**: A neutral Cayman foundation company authorized by OpenGov referenda to execute off-chain legal contracts, pay fiat salaries, and hold commercial software licenses.

Polkadot's Treasury is funded directly through protocol-level rules: transaction fees, unspent token issuance, and **validator slashing penalties** flow directly into the OpenGov Treasury pool.

## 2. Core Precedents & Empirical Mechanics

### 2.1 Polkadot Technical Fellowship (Rank 0–9 Ladder)
- **Mechanics**: Core Substrate/Polkadot developers belong to an on-chain Fellowship structured across 10 distinct ranks (Rank 0 Candidate to Rank 9 Grand Master). Rank promotion requires peer evaluation and proven code contributions. Fellowship members receive automated monthly stipends scaled to their rank.
- **Empirical Impact**: Eliminates political grant voting for core developers, establishing a predictable, rank-based maintainer stipend ladder.

### 2.2 Polkadot Community Foundation (PCF Legal Wrapper)
- **Mechanics**: On-chain DAOs cannot directly sign legal contracts or execute fiat transactions. Polkadot established the PCF as a legal execution layer governed by OpenGov direction to execute off-chain maintainer agreements and vendor SLAs.

### 2.3 Slashing & Penalty Routing
- **Mechanics**: When validators violate consensus or equivocate, a designated percentage of the slashed DOT is routed automatically to the Treasury rather than being burned.
