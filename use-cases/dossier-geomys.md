## Mechanism (how money / governance / maintenance actually works)

Geomys is a **small private firm of professional open-source maintainers**, founded by Filippo Valsorda (announced 8 Jul 2024). Clients pay a **fixed monthly retainer** for (1) sustainable, predictable maintenance of a **shared thematic Go portfolio**, and (2) **direct access** to maintainer expertise (framed as a fractional internal Go team / Slack Connect channels). The portfolio is a package deal — clients do not apportion value per project.

**Associate Maintainers** receive stable guaranteed income plus a negotiated cut of future retainer growth; Geomys consolidates admin, legal, sales, and support staff. Blueprint essay: no copyright assignment, no locked-in term, no GitHub ownership transfer; firm takes **no outside investment** (financed from existing cash flow); growth is intended to stay Go-thematic, not “Open Source Maintainers, Inc.”

Public **Standard of Care** (GitHub) states commitments offered to the **whole community**, not only paying clients. Paying clients do **not** get private vulnerability details ahead of public disclosure. Separately, Geomys sells **FIPS 140-3 Go Cryptographic Module** commercial/enterprise packages (CMVP validation + enterprise attestations) with **posted list prices** on `geomys.org/fips140`.

**Named maintainers on geomys.org (checked 2026-09-24 PT):** Filippo Valsorda; Nicola Murino (`x/crypto/ssh`); Dominik Honnef (Staticcheck, Gotraceui); Daniel McCarney; Daniel Morsing. Blueprint also names Matilde Dal Zilio (Administrative Director).

**Portfolio examples on geomys.org / Standard of Care:** Go stdlib `crypto/...` and `golang.org/x/crypto/...` (co-maintained with Google Go team); FIPS 140-3 Go Cryptographic Module; `filippo.io/...` crypto packages; Staticcheck; Gotraceui; bluemonday; age; mkcert; sandboxed-step; plus additional `filippo.io/*` / Sunlight / yubikey-agent items on the Standard of Care scope list.

**General portfolio retainer sticker price:** **UNKNOWN** (not published on geomys.org homepage or blueprint essay). Contact is `hi@` on the geomys.org domain.

## What would transfer (honest judgment)

Geomys is a **Stage 0 research candidate** for **OMF retainer architecture** and a **Family B-adjacent** commercial assurance wedge (especially FIPS), not a closed-loop ORF. What transfers is the **legible product** (clients buy portfolio sustainability + expertise access), the **Standard of Care** as public commitment language, and proof that some enterprises will pay recurring cash for maintenance of critical dependencies. What does **not** transfer without new design work: Neutral Entity custody, an ecosystem cost floor with PCR/Gates, diversified replenishment families, or non-vendor governance. Treat as **P-scale external precedent for maintainer employment**, local **D0** until any adopter publishes receipts against its own floor.
