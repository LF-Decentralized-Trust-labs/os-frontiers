## 1. Investment Objectives & Capital Preservation
- **Primary Objective**: Preserve capital in real terms while generating non-inflationary, counter-cyclical yield to cover baseline open-source maintenance costs.
- **Secondary Objective**: Maintain liquid reserves equal to at least **[24–36 months]** of baseline OMF operational expenditure.
- **Spending Rule**: Only net investment income and yield above target inflation may be distributed; principal reserves remain intact.

---

## 2. Asset Allocation & Exposure Limits

| Asset Class | Target Allocation | Maximum Limit | Permitted Assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Cash & Liquid Buffers** | 30% | 50% | Native Stables (USDC, USDT), Fiat Escrow |
| **Tier 2: Staked & Native Yield** | 40% | 60% | Staked ETH/ADA, Governed Liquid Staking Tokens |
| **Tier 3: Low-Risk DeFi & Fixed Income** | 20% | 30% | Short-term T-Bill tokens, Aave/Compound stable sleeves |
| **Tier 4: Strategic & Risk Mutuals** | 10% | 15% | Nexus Mutual capital pools, Reciprocal venture sleeves |

> 🛑 *Prohibited Assets*: High-beta meme tokens, un-audited algorithmic stablecoins, leveraged perpetuals, and un-sanctioned yield bridges.

---

## 3. Manager Powers & Replacement Rules
- **Manager Appointment**: Financial managers are appointed via dOSPO mandate for a maximum 12-month renewable term.
- **Manager Replaceability**: Governance retains non-discretionary authority to terminate any investment manager or revoke smart-contract vault delegate powers upon a 51% community vote.
- **Concentration Limit**: No single asset manager, protocol vault, or custodian may hold more than **25%** of total endowment assets.

---

## 4. Risk Governance & Reporting
- **Monthly Reporting**: Published disclosures detailing portfolio net asset value (NAV), gross yield, management fees, drawdowns, and smart-contract exposure.
- **Emergency Suspension**: Automatic de-risking trigger if portfolio drawdown exceeds **10%** in a 30-day rolling window.
