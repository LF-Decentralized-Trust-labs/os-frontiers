# ORF Replenishment Posture Evaluation Sheet

> **The 5-Question Framework for Assessing Ecosystem Replenishment Readiness**  
> *Excerpted from the Open Replenishment Framework (ORF) July 2026 Validated Edition*

---

## Instructions
Score your ecosystem honestly across the 5 core questions below (0–5 points each, total score out of 25). Use the scoring rubric to locate your replenishment layer and identify the next collection instruments to implement.

---

## Assessment Rubric

### Question 1: Ratio
*What is your replenishment ratio today, and what subsidizes the gap?*
- **0 Points**: No replenishment; 100% dependent on unmeasured reserve drawdown or continuous token dilution with no path to sustainability.
- **3 Points**: Replenishment ratio measured; partial fee splits or voluntary contributions cover 10-30% of maintenance costs, gap is named.
- **5 Points**: Healthy. Replenishment ratio ≥ 1.0 or non-inflationary income covers baseline maintenance, and reserve usage is scheduled as a repayable bootstrap loan.

---

### Question 2: Anchors
*Does each collection instrument attach to a fork-resistant source of value rather than permission to use code?*
- **0 Points**: Collection relies on charging for open-source code access or easily copyable license restrictions.
- **3 Points**: Collection attaches to native token fees or basic brand placement, but lacks canonical liquidity or SLA anchors.
- **5 Points**: Healthy. Avoidance requires giving up a separately valuable service, canonical state/liquidity, registry certification, or support relationship.

---

### Question 3: Bundles
*Is every optional payment attached to a benefit the payer independently wants?*
- **0 Points**: Payments are framed as mandatory taxes or un-bundled donations with no tangible counter-value.
- **3 Points**: Basic sponsorship badges offered, but lack structured SLAs, LTS patch windows, or dependency intelligence.
- **5 Points**: Healthy. Sold, not taxed. Payers receive enterprise SLAs, patch commitments, or registry placement while security intake remains universal.

---

### Question 4: Mandate
*Is each collection instrument chartered, time-bounded, and publicly reported like a spending program?*
- **0 Points**: Revenue collection is ad-hoc, un-chartered, or managed in opaque accounts without community authorization.
- **3 Points**: Governance authorized collection, but lacks time-limited charters or independent cost-to-collect auditing.
- **5 Points**: Healthy. Every collection rail carries a dOSPO mandate, sunset clause, cost-to-collect budget, and independent audit review.

---

### Question 5: Runway
*Can baseline maintenance survive a full 2-3 year bear market without an emergency reserve draw?*
- **0 Points**: Maintenance budget collapses immediately if native token price drops by 50%+.
- **3 Points**: 12-18 months of runway in stable assets, but high price correlation and no productive capital sleeve.
- **5 Points**: Healthy. Conservative, diversified non-inflationary income (SLA subscriptions + IPS endowment yield) covers baseline floor after costs.

---

## Total Score & Readiness Level

| Total Score | Readiness Stage | Recommended Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| **0 – 9 Points** | **Phase 1: Reserve-Funded (Bootstrap)** | Establish dOSPO mandate; launch Enterprise SLA pilot & 5-Q posture baseline. |
| **10 – 18 Points** | **Phase 2: Fee-Supplemented** | Scale SLA sales; test dependency attestation pools & ecosystem-owned services. |
| **19 – 25 Points** | **Phase 3: Self-Sustaining** | Deploy Capital IPS sleeve & yield routing; target Net Replenishment Ratio ≥ 1.0. |
