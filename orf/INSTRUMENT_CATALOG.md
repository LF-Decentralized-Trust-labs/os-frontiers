## Layer-by-Layer Instrument Breakdown

### 1. Protocol Layer (Automatic Capture)
Instruments encoded directly into core consensus, block building, or sequencing rules. They capture value automatically from ledger activity without requiring per-transaction user consent.
* **Key Precedents**: Cardano Monetary Policy, Optimism Sequencer Tithe, Polkadot Slashing.
* **Governance Guardrail**: Must carry constitutional community mandates due to their proximity to taxation.

### 2. Application Layer (Incentivized Opt-In)
Instruments that capture value from applications operating on top of shared infrastructure.
* **Flagship Proposal**: "Sustains the Commons" Certification. Apps that route a disclosed fee share to the treasury receive official badge placement, priority support, and registry verification.
* **Dependency Metering**: Drips graph splitting and tea attestation pools.

### 3. Enterprise & Services Layer (Earned Revenue)
Commercial products sold directly to enterprises, denominated in fiat or stablecoins.
* **Core Products**: Maintenance SLAs, Extended Lifecycle Support (LTS) patch windows, dependency security intelligence, corporate training.
* **Reciprocal Clauses**: Commercial grants that convert into capped revenue shares or repayable advances upon commercial success.

### 4. Capital Layer (Productive Treasury)
Transforms static treasury reserves into active, yield-generating income assets governed by an **Investment Policy Statement (IPS)**.
* **Core Products**: Governed Endowments (ENS IPS), Yield-donating stablecoins (Glo Dollar), Security Mutuals (Nexus Mutual).

### 5. Delegation Layer (Ecosystem Extension)
Behavioral layer for Proof-of-Stake networks where pool operators pledge a portion of margin rewards to public-goods maintenance.
