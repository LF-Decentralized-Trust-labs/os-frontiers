## 1. Scope of Maintenance Services (Benefit Bundle)
The Provider grants the Client access to the following enterprise-grade maintenance services for designated open-source repositories:
- **Defined Patch Windows**: Backported security fixes for release lines up to **[24/36 months]** post-release.
- **SLA Response Targets**: Severity 1 incidents acknowledged within **[2 hours]**; resolution path within **[24 hours]**.
- **Compatibility Guidance**: Pre-release API breaking change notices and dependency compatibility matrices.
- **Official Certification**: Inclusion in the official "[Ecosystem Name] Sustains the Commons" enterprise registry.

> ⚠️ *Public Safety Exemption*: Public disclosure of security vulnerabilities and standard patch availability for critical bugs remain open to the general public under open-source license terms.

---

## 2. Reciprocal Funding & Return Clause (Optional Commercial Sleeve)
Where the Provider supplies treasury grant funding or co-development engineering resources to Client's commercial initiatives:
- **Capped Revenue Share**: Client agrees to route **[1.0% – 3.0%]** of net commercial application revenue generated by the funded repository back to the Provider's sustainability treasury.
- **Cap Limit**: Total reciprocal payments under this clause shall be capped at **[150% – 200%]** of total grant funds received.
- **Repayment Trigger**: Reciprocal payments commence 12 months after commercial product launch and are reported quarterly.

---

## 3. Maintenance Autonomy Safeguard
Client acknowledges that payment under this agreement purchases technical support services and reciprocal financial obligations; **it does not grant Client technical control, veto power, or roadmap authority over open-source repositories**.
