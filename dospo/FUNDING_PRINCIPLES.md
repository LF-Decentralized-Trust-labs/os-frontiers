# dOSPO Funding Principles

> **Core Rules Governing Capital Allocation & Operator Accountability**  
> *Author: Christian Taylor · Open Source Frontiers Lab · LF Decentralized Trust*

---

## 1. Principles of Capital Allocation

1. **Separation of Policy & Execution**: Governance sets policy boundaries; dOSPO operators execute allocations without discretionary political influence.
2. **Neutrality Guarantee**: Funding allocation is strictly vendor-neutral. Corporate sponsorship never grants technical veto power over open-source roadmaps.
3. **Accountability & Replaceability**: Operators are evaluated quarterly. Governance retains the right to revoke operator mandates via referendum.
4. **Transparency**: All allocations, retainers, and audit findings are published publicly under standardized quarterly reporting templates.
