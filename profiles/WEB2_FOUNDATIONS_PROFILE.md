## 1. Overview & Institutional Precedents

Web2 open-source foundations demonstrate how legal hosting, project governance, training, and corporate sponsorship can be separated from technical code authority.

---

## 2. Key Framework Insights

### 1. Enterprise Maintenance SLAs (Red Hat & Tidelift)
- Customers purchase defined patch windows, supported release branches, compatibility guarantees, and supply-chain assurance for openly licensed software.
- Demonstrates **Benefit Bundling** in its most proven commercial form.

### 2. Separation of Sponsorship and Technical Authority (Apache)
- Apache explicitly separates financial sponsorship from PMC (Project Management Committee) technical voting rights.
- Precedent for preventing enterprise funding from purchasing roadmap control.

### 3. Corporate OSPOs as Enterprise Interfaces
- Corporate OSPOs aggregate enterprise demand, purchase assurance SLAs, sponsor shared infrastructure, and supply dependency data to neutral foundations.
