## 1. Primary Function & Capabilities
Safe is the industry-standard programmable smart-account multisig infrastructure used to secure DAO treasuries and execute governance calls.

## 2. Role in dOSPO / OMF / ORF Suite
- **dOSPO Vault Control**: Provides the secure multisig custody layer holding community treasury reserves and enforcing policy limits.
- **SafeSnap Governance**: Connects Snapshot off-chain governance votes to automated on-chain transaction execution.
