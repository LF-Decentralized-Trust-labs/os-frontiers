## 1. Primary Function & Capabilities
Octant routes staking rewards generated from 100,000 staked ETH to community-selected open-source public goods projects.

## 2. Role in dOSPO / OMF / ORF Suite
- **ORF Capital Layer Precedent**: Demonstrates how governed staking yield can create a non-inflationary replenishment inflow.
- **Operating-Cost Anchor**: Publicly allocates a 25% share of yield to foundation operating costs — a real-world anchor for cost-to-collect modeling.
