## 1. Overview & Architecture

The Cosmos ecosystem relies on modular Cosmos SDK chains connected via Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC). Governance and public goods maintenance are funded through chain-specific community pools and sub-DAOs.

---

## 2. Framework Mapping

### dOSPO (Governance & Delegation)
- **ATOM Accelerator DAO (AADAO)**: A sub-DAO authorized by Cosmos Hub governance to manage grant allocations for open-source public goods, core Cosmos SDK infrastructure, and IBC maintenance.
- **Community Pool Governance**: A fixed percentage of inflationary block rewards and transaction fees is continuously directed to the Community Pool.

### OMF (Maintenance Deployment)
- **Developer Grants & RFP Streams**: Funding core Cosmos SDK components (CometBFT, IBC-go, CosmWasm) via managed grants and DoraHacks quadratic funding rounds.

### ORF (Collection & Replenishment)
- **Interchain Public Goods Delegation**: Validator pools pledge margin rewards to open-source maintainers across IBC chains, surfaced via neutral interchain registries.
