## 1. Overview & Architecture

Solana uses the high-performance **Sealevel** parallel execution engine. Governance and treasury management rely on SPL Governance (**Realms**) and smart account multisig infrastructure (**Squads**).

---

## 2. Framework Mapping

### dOSPO (Governance Authority)
- **Realms (SPL Governance)**: On-chain DAO governance platform managing proposals, voting weight, and token-based governance instruction execution.
- **Squads Protocol**: On-chain smart account multisig infrastructure acting as the secure execution layer for developer grants, treasury assets, and program upgrades.

### OMF (Maintenance Deployment)
- **Solana Foundation Grants & R.E.D. Program**: Direct funding for core developer tooling (Anchor framework, Solita, Metaplex standards) and RPC infrastructure.

### ORF (Collection & Replenishment)
- **Enterprise SLAs**: Managed RPC providers and core tooling developers offer enterprise SLA support packages denominated in SOL or stablecoins.
