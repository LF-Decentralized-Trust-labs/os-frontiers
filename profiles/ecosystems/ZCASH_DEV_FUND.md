## 1. Overview & Empirical Lessons

Zcash provides one of the longest-running empirical records of **Protocol-Layer collection governance**:
- **ZIP 1014 (2020–2024)**: Directed 20% of block subsidy (7% ECC, 5% Zcash Foundation, 8% Zcash Community Grants) with miners retaining 80%.
- **Expiry by Default**: The charter expired by default at the November 2024 halving unless affirmatively renewed — validating the ORF recommendation for time-limited charters.
- **ZIP 1015 / ZIP 2001 / ZIP 1016**: NU6 implemented ZIP 1015 (8% ZCG, 12% Deferred Dev Fund Lockbox). ZIP 2001 defined the lockbox, while ZIP 1016/271 propose moving lockbox funds toward coinholder governance.

---

## 2. Key Framework Insights
1. **Mandate Legitimacy**: Demonstrates that protocol collection survives only when authorization can be revisited separately from spending allocations.
2. **Governance Cost**: Highlights the political contention and runway uncertainty that occurs when developer funding relies on a periodically contested protocol parameter.
