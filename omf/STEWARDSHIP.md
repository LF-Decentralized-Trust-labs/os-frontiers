# OMF Stewardship & Dependency Management Guide

> **Portfolio Management Protocols for Open Source Dependencies & Critical Infrastructure**  
> *Author: Christian Taylor · Open Source Frontiers Lab · LF Decentralized Trust*

---

## 1. Principles of Open Stewardship

The **Open Maintenance Framework (OMF)** views open-source repositories as a shared portfolio of public goods requiring continuous stewardship rather than one-off grants.

---

## 2. Dependency Graph Auditing

Every repository under OMF stewardship must complete a quarterly [Dependency Audit](./DEPENDENCY_AUDIT_TEMPLATE.md) to identify:
- Single-Point-of-Failure (SPOF) dependencies.
- Maintainer Bus Factors and burnout risks.
- Upstream security patch requirements.
