## Draft

The Open Source Frontiers Lab, a lab project hosted at LF Decentralized Trust, published a Stage 0 design for a problem treasuries already have: they can fund maintenance for years and still have no path for that value to come back.

The design is three specifications. dOSPO is who decides. OMF is how money goes out. ORF is how money comes back. None of the eight hard gates is marked passed. A score from the evaluator is not a pass.

The repository is <https://github.com/LF-Decentralized-Trust-labs/os-frontiers>. The LF Decentralized Trust labs page is <https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/projects/labs>. This draft does not claim the lab is listed on that page.

A treasury that wants the short version can start at the quickstart in the repository. A researcher can start at START_HERE. Comparison profiles of other organizations are in the landscape section. They are evidence notes, not endorsements.
