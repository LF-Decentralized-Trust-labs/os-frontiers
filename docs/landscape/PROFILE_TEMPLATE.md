## Rules

1. **Link, don't copy.** Cite the publisher's URL. Do not commit copies of third-party pages, PDFs or reports.
2. **Exact figures only.** Write a number only if the cited page states it. Otherwise write **Unverified**.
3. **Label the figure's class.** A site counter, a press release and a Form 990 are not the same kind of evidence.
4. **Keep ORF terms strict.** Grants, pledges and payout platforms move money; they are not replenishment unless the money recurs from a source the profiled body earns or holds. Routing rails are labelled as rails.
5. **Record conflicts.** When two sources disagree, give both, say which one this profile uses, and why.
6. **Neutrality.** No consulting, vendor or sales material. Profiles describe; they do not recommend a vendor.
7. **Paraphrase.** Quote at most a few words, and only when exact wording matters.
