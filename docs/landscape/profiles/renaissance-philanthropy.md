## What they do

Renaissance Philanthropy is a US philanthropic organization that hosts funds backed by other donors. In May 2026 it launched the Open Source for Science Fund to sustain and evolve the open source software that research depends on. The first call, "Open Source for the Life Sciences", has two tracks: domain-specific tools and foundational libraries. It also co-runs Project Odin with the Ethereum Foundation, already profiled in this repo.
