## What they do

A US 501(c)(3) charity, incorporated on 14 February 2025, that invests donations and spends only the returns on grants to critical open source projects without strong corporate ties. Donors of $1,000 or more a year become members who elect the board. It is the only player in this landscape whose core model is replenishment by capital income.
