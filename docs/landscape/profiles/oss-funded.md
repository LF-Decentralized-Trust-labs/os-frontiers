## What they do

A newsletter, published on Buttondown with an archive at ossfunded.com, that collects open source funding news each week: foundation fundraising, public programs, corporate commitments, venture rounds and licensing changes. Treat it as a lead list. Every item should be traced to its primary source before it goes into a profile.
