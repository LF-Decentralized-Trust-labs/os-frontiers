## What they do

A US non-profit that hosts many open source projects and sub-foundations, including OpenSSF, Alpha-Omega, CHAOSS and LF Decentralized Trust. Its largest income source is company memberships and donations (about 43% of 2025 forecast revenue), followed by project services, events, and training and certification. It is the largest working example of the membership-dues model that ORF calls Family C. It also publishes research on open source funding (Census III, the 2024 funding report).

## OSF fit

| OSF layer | What maps | What does not |
|---|---|---|
| dOSPO | Neutral legal host; each project has its own charter and governing board | Boards are weighted toward paying members |
| OMF | Project support budgets, staff and infrastructure | Little direct pay to individual maintainers |
| ORF | Family C at scale: recurring dues; plus earned income from training and events | Dues depend on corporate budgets; very little flows to individual maintainers |

## Gaps and cautions

- The four revenue categories add to about $303m, about $8m short of the $311m total. The press report does not explain the gap.
- Kernel spending figures disagree between outlets ($6.7m vs $8.4m). Read the annual report itself before citing.
- The 2025 figures are a forecast published in December 2025.
