## What they do

A Dutch foundation that regrants European Commission money to open source work on internet freedom, privacy, open standards and decentralization. For about a decade it ran the NGI Zero programmes under the Commission's Next Generation Internet initiative. In 2026 it paused most open calls to take stock of NGI Zero and prepare three successor programmes under the "Open Internet Stack": Restack, CodeSupply and ELFA. Two themed funds, NGI Taler (privacy-preserving payments) and NGI Fediversity (hosting), kept accepting proposals.

## Gaps and cautions

- NLnet's pages contradict themselves on what is open: the article says only Taler and Fediversity accept proposals, while the sidebar lists Restack and CodeSupply as open. One page also shows two different deadlines (1 August and 3 November 2026). Check the live call page before citing.
- The pause shows the main risk of this model: a gap between Commission funding cycles stops new grants.
