## What they do

The Digital Commons European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (DC EDIC) is an intergovernmental body of EU member states, based in Paris, set up to develop and maintain digital commons for Europe. Its first two projects are a "100 Day Challenge" to build sovereign, interoperable open source components and a pilot European Sovereign Tech Fund (EU-STF), developed with Germany's Sovereign Tech Agency. The idea of an EU-wide fund was argued for earlier by OpenForum Europe in a study backed by several partners, including Mercedes-Benz.

## Gaps and cautions

- Sources disagree on the founding date: one says October 2025, another December 2025.
- No budget has been published. Do not treat the €264 billion figure in the Matrix.org post as EDIC money; it is the Commission's estimate of EU spending on proprietary products.
- The Matrix.org post is written by an advocate for this agenda.
