## What they do

A program announced by the Commonhaus Foundation on 2 July 2026. When a Commonhaus project declares a version end-of-life, vetted partners can provide ongoing security fixes to users who cannot upgrade yet. Partners must hold an active Gold or Silver sponsorship, SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 certification, and prior CVE work on relevant end-of-life software. The program gives partners no governance rights or say over project direction. HeroDevs is the founding Gold Partner.

## Gaps and cautions

- This is one of the few live programs that resembles ORF's certification-plus-assurance design. It is new; check the program page for prices and whether any money is passed to projects before citing it as replenishment.
