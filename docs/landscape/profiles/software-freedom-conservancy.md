## What they do

A US 501(c)(3) non-profit, founded in 2006 in New York, that gives free and open source projects a legal and financial home without each forming its own company. It holds copyrights and trademarks for member projects, manages their money and contributor agreements, and enforces the GPL. It also runs the Outreachy internship program, which moved to Conservancy from GNOME in 2015. Member projects include Git, QEMU, Inkscape, Samba, Wine, BusyBox and Boost.

## Gaps and cautions

- Current member count and finances were not found on a primary page this pass. Use the latest Form 990 before citing money figures.
- Its stance differs from industry foundations: it campaigns on licensing and against some platforms (for example its 2022 "Give Up GitHub" campaign). That is a values choice, not a gap.
