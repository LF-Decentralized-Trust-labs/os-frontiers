## What they do

A Linux Foundation project that brings companies together to improve open source security. It is funded by member dues. Its Technical Advisory Council funds "technical initiatives": working groups and projects that build shared security tools and practices, such as scorecards and supply-chain standards. It co-manages the March 2026 $12.5m commitment with Alpha-Omega.
