## What they do

An application-monitoring company that has paid open source maintainers every year since 2021 and founded the Open Source Pledge. It publishes a yearly breakdown of where the money went. Most of it is routed by dependency through thanks.dev; the rest goes through Ecosyste.ms Funds, GitHub Sponsors and direct gifts to foundations. It was also the first funder of Ecosyste.ms Funds.

## Gaps and cautions

- The 2025 channel figures add up to $718,750, not $750,000. The post does not explain the $31,250 difference.
- The same post says Microsoft "mothballed" GitHub Sponsors, meaning new feature work stopped. GitHub's own July 2026 post presents Sponsors as growing. See [`github-sponsors.md`](./github-sponsors.md).
