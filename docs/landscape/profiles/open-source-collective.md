## What they do

A non-profit umbrella organization that acts as the legal and financial home for open source projects on the Open Collective platform. A hosted project can take donations, pay contributors and file taxes without forming its own company. Open Source Collective handles accounting, payments, tax reporting, invoices and legal liability coverage, and runs the payout side of Ecosyste.ms Funds.

## Gaps and cautions

- The general Open Collective fee page lets each host set its own fee; the 10% figure is Open Source Collective's, from its own application page.
- The legal form (for example a US 501(c)(6)) is not stated on the page opened. Check before citing.
