## What they do

A US private foundation whose Technology program includes an "Open Source in Science" sub-program. It does not mainly fund individual software projects. It funds the tooling, institutions, economic models and incentives around research software: university open source program offices, publishing and archiving, and career paths for research software engineers. It has funded CHAOSS (community health metrics) and is a funding partner of GitHub's Secure Open Source Fund. It also co-funded the Ford Foundation's critical digital infrastructure research in 2019.

## Gaps and cautions

- The sub-program names have changed over time (an earlier "Better Software for Science" sub-program appears in older grants). The description above comes from a February 2026 university notice, not from Sloan's own site. Check sloan.org before citing current program names.
- Total open source giving is not published as one figure; the grants database lists grants one at a time.
