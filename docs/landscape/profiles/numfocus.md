## What they do

A US 501(c)(3) public charity that is the fiscal sponsor for many open source scientific computing projects, especially in the Python data stack that much AI work depends on. It provides legal services (licensing, trademarks, contracts), financial services (grant administration, contractor agreements, taxes) and operational support. It runs Small Development Grants three times a year for its projects, funded by individual donors and corporate sponsors. Sentry describes it as managing many of the Python projects at the heart of the modern AI stack.
