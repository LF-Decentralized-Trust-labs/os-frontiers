## What they do

A GitHub feature, launched in 2019, that lets individuals and organizations sponsor developers and projects directly, monthly or once. Organization sponsorship became generally available in 2023, with invoicing and bulk sponsorship added later. Other programs use it as their payout rail, including GitHub's Secure Open Source Fund and the Rust Foundation Maintainers Fund.

## Gaps and cautions

- **Conflicting signals about the product's future.** Sentry's January 2026 post says Microsoft "mothballed" Sponsors (new feature work stopped). GitHub's July 2026 post reports accelerating growth. Both can be true: money flowing can grow while product investment stalls. Cite each with its date.
- Some of the money is maintainers sponsoring each other, a point raised in Open Source Pledge interviews. The $100m figure does not separate this out.
- Fees are not stated in the milestone post.
