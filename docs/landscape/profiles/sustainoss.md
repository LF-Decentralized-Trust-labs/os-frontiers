## What they do

A community of people working on open source sustainability, describing itself as a space for "open source sustainers". It runs the Sustain Summit and satellite events, working groups that produce guidance, a Discourse forum, and the Sustain podcast, hosted by Richard Littauer and others. It also keeps an academic map of research groups in the field, for example its CHAOSS entry. It is an ecosystem partner of GitHub's Secure Open Source Fund.
