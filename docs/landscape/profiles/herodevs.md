## What they do

A company that sells "Never-Ending Support": paid security updates for open source versions that projects have declared end-of-life. It says it donates part of that revenue back to open source. In June 2025 it announced a $20m Open Source Sustainability Fund for maintainers who follow good security practice when they retire versions. It is a Pledge member, a funding partner of GitHub's Secure Open Source Fund, and the founding Gold Partner of Commonhaus's end-of-life support program. It raised $125m from the investment firm PSG in July 2025.

## Gaps and cautions

- **The 2026 figure changed.** An earlier search excerpt of the same page showed $1,738,729 (about $47,000 per developer). The page fetched on 27 October 2026 shows $739,137 ($19,977 per developer, 37 developers). The Pledge member list (9 October 2026) shows 50 developers at $3,200 each, probably an older report. This profile uses the page as fetched; say which version you cite.
- The fund's $20m is a commitment over an unstated period. Paid-out totals and recipients are not published.
- A company that sells support for end-of-life versions benefits when projects retire versions in an orderly way. That is not a problem in itself, but it should be stated.
