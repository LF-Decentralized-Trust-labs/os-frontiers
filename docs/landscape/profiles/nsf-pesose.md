## What they do

A US National Science Foundation funding opportunity announced on 9 June 2026. It pays for organizations that manage open source ecosystems, rather than for individual projects. It has three tracks: scoping and planning a new ecosystem; building a sustainable ecosystem around a promising open source product; and improving the security and privacy of an existing ecosystem. The page says it builds on earlier NSF programs in this area.
