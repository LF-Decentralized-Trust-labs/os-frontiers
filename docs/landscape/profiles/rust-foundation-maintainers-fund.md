## What they do

A fund for paying existing Rust Project maintainers to maintain critical parts of Rust, such as the compiler, standard library, Cargo and Clippy. Its main program is "Maintainer in Residence", expected to be a near full-time role with stable, long-term funding; smaller grants are also possible. A Funding team created by Rust RFC 3931 chooses roles based on money available and maintenance needs. The fund was announced by the Rust Foundation and launched publicly by the Funding team on 2 June 2026, on behalf of the Rust Leadership Council.
