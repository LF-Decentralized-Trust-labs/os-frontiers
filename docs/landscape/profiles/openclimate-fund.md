## What they do

A small initiative of OpenSustain.tech (a catalogue of open source climate and sustainability projects) and ecosyste.ms. A contributor adds a climate or sustainability project to the OpenSustain.tech list through a pull request, then names a different listed project to receive money. After review, the initiative sends €100 to the chosen project through that project's own funding platform. The model rewards building the catalogue and spreads small sums across a field that has few dedicated funders.
