Q3 2026 Membership Audit

August 26, 2026

Our membership has concluded its latest quarterly audit of funded members as of August 5, 2026. This, our 17th consecutive update reaffirms our institution's consistency and ability to self-regulate.

The member registry now includes a number of new contributor accounts as well as new alumni; creating a representative body of the developers and maintainers who work on client implementations, research, and upgrade delivery.

Protocol Guild's membership is now 190, with a net decrease of 6 (-3.1%) members from 196 of last quarter. This quarter, the membership update was once again processed onchain via Protocol Guild's Agora-based DAO contracts. Readers will find a comprehensive list of the changeset below.
