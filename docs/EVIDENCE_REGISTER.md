## 1. Executive Summary & Research Conclusions

This register indexes governance records, legal-entity descriptions, and financial disclosures for the precedents below. It is not an audit-ready certificate. Findings that are judgments are labeled as judgments.

### Key Research Findings

1. **No Reviewed Ecosystem Implements the Complete Closed Loop Under These Names**: A judgment about the rows in this register, not a measured fact. Not re-scored in this pass. Confidence: judgment, not a measurement.
2. **ENS, two snapshots, not one cell**. The KPK 2025 review, posted 21 January 2026, reports operational revenue of $18.22M, operational expenses of $17.54M, endowment revenues covering 20.3% of those expenses, and December assets under management of $113,86M (the page's European comma). Passed [EP 6.46](https://docs.ens.domains/dao/proposals/6.46/) states a later print: approximately $93.4M in non-custodial assets under management, over $8M in net DeFi returns since inception, and 2025 operating expenses of $16.4M per Steakhouse. The AUM footnote on that page is `period=apr_2026`. Same institution, two closes. Confidence: **Medium**.
3. **Polkadot Community Foundation**. The wiki page describes a Cayman Islands foundation company whose activities are directed by DOT holders via OpenGov: an unopinionated executor, no shareholders, able to sign contracts and make fiat payments, with token holders able to withhold funding, remove the supervisor or directors, amend bylaws, or wind up the foundation. Treasury-spend totals and referendum amounts are **unverified** in this pass. Confidence: **Sourced** for that legal design only.
4. **Cardano POSM**. The Intersect explainer describes OSC oversight, OSO execution, maintainer retainers, Code for Us, incubation, and a model that "supports commercial adoption, replenishing the treasury." It does not state a 2025 ADA budget or a bounty-pool total. Those magnitudes are **unverified**. The Cardano monetary-policy page was also opened and does not state them. Confidence: **Sourced** for the program description; **Unverified** for budget magnitudes.
5. **Optimism Superchain**. Current docs state the standard-chain rule as the greater of 15% of net transaction-fee profit or 2.5% of gross transaction fees, and state that OP Mainnet contributes 100% of its revenue to the shared treasury. The 24 June 2025 Year 3 budget update reports 17,756 ETH in all-time Superchain revenue, primarily driven by Base revenue share (6,210 ETH) and OP Mainnet (11,170 ETH), and 26.4M OP committed via Retro Funding across 437 grantees. Those two chain figures do not sum to the all-time total; the post does not itemize the residual. The same post says the Foundation budget comes from the initial 30% supply and is not subject to governance approval. The 8 January 2026 Foundation post is a proposal, not the vote: 50% of incoming Superchain revenue, and 5,868 ETH collected over the preceding twelve months. The onchain vote page linked in section 3 is marked SUCCEEDED, ended 6:58 pm on 28 January 2026, and describes 50% of incoming Superchain revenue for a twelve-month program beginning in February. CoinDesk, published 28 January 2026, reports the proposal passed with 84.4% in favor and describes the allocation as 50% of net Superchain sequencer revenue. Confidence: **Medium**. Not an independent audit.

## 4. Mandatory 12-Field Revenue Tracking Rules

To prevent misclassifying grant distributions, token dilution, or temporary spikes as sustainable revenue, every ORF collection report must track twelve mandatory fields:

1. **Gross Cash Received**: Actual USD/stablecoin receipts collected (excludes un-realized token pledges).
2. **Collection & Service Cost**: Itemized sales, legal, tax, and support delivery overhead.
3. **Net Contribution**: Net capital transferred to the Governed Treasury ($R_{\text{net}} = R_{\text{gross}} - C_{\text{ops}}$).
4. **Source Classification**: Protocol, Enterprise, Voluntary, Investment, Issuance, or Routing Rail.
5. **Recurring vs One-Time**: Distinguishes multi-year subscriptions/fees from episodic grants.
6. **Contract Term & Termination**: Captures exit rights and contract duration.
7. **Payer Concentration Ratio ($\text{RCR}$)**: Share of inflows originating from the largest single payer ($\le 0.25$).
8. **Native-Token Exposure**: Correlation factor with native token market price.
9. **Customer Renewal Rate**: Verified percentage of commercial subscribers renewing contracts.
10. **Outstanding Service Liabilities**: Un-fulfilled customer SLA obligations and refund reserves.
11. **Restricted / Unrestricted Status**: Accounting classification for treasury use.
12. **Audit & Provenance Status**: Third-party verification details and transaction hashes.

### Contractual Exit & Concentration Risk Formula
$$\text{Exit Risk Score} = \text{RCR} \times \text{Contract Durability} \times \text{Exit Rights} \times \text{Economic Correlation} \times \text{Enforceability}$$
*Directly derived from Optimism's Base stack transition, where a single large payer's stack migration altered protocol fee routing.*
