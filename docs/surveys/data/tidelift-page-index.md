## Caveats found while indexing (source-internal; report as-is, do not "fix")

1. **2024 p40 vs p59:** 60% quit-or-considered (p40) vs "almost two-thirds" (p59). Cite p40.
2. **2024 p4 vs p13:** 60% unpaid hobbyist (single-choice) vs 47% "don't get paid" (multi-select). Both are correct answers to different questions; quote the one matching the claim.
3. **2023 p26:** 52% and 53% for "not compensated" on the same page. The 2024 report (p38) uses 52%.
4. **2023 p31 vs p32:** "Earning more income" 56% (chart) vs 57% (text).
5. **n captions on the quit question are inconsistent across editions:** 2023 p28 prints "2023: n=349; 2021: n=265" and p29 prints "2021: n=349; 2023: n=265"; 2024 p40 prints "n=265 (2023)". Treat 2023 quit-question n as UNVERIFIED (265 or 349).
6. **2021 dislikes n:** the 2021 report (p15) and the 2023 caption (p25) give n=335; the 2024 report's chart caption (p38) prints "n=253 (2021)", which matches 2023's n and looks like a caption error. Use 335.
7. **Quit reasons were not asked in 2024.** The 2024 "50% / 48% / 43%" figures are *dislikes*, not quit reasons. The latest quit-reasons data is 2023 (p29).
8. **"Users are too demanding", 2021 vs 2023.** 2021 report p15: 37% (2021). 2023 chart p25 shows only a delta, "-1%" (i.e. 36% in 2023). 2024 chart p38 (word positions checked) prints 37 (2021), 36 (2023), 39 (2024). The 2023 *text* p26 says "37% of respondents (36% in previous survey)", i.e. the two years swapped. Three sources against one: use 37% (2021), 36% (2023), 39% (2024).
9. **"Not compensated", 2023.** The 2023 chart p25 shows "+3%" over 2021's 49%, i.e. 52%; the 2024 chart p38 prints 52 for 2023. The "53%" in 2023 p26 text is the outlier. Use 52%.
10. All editions are self-selected samples recruited via Tidelift channels and social media; per-question n varies (shown per row).
