## Overview

### Summary
**Project Odin** ([https://projectodin.org/](https://projectodin.org/)) is an initiative by the **Ethereum Foundation's Funding Coordination team** in partnership with **Renaissance Philanthropy** (powered by ARIA UK / FRC Launchpad). It operates as a support program and practical laboratory designed to help strategic Ethereum Digital Public Goods (DPGs) transition from single-source grant dependency into sustainable "Frontier Research Contractors" (FRCs) with credible, diversified, long-term earned revenue pathways.

### Purpose
Project Odin addresses the fundamental question:  
> *"Can we translate research into deliverables clients can pay for?"*

The program assists selected EF grantee teams in building commercial capabilities, architecting compliant legal/financial containers, and converting technical expertise into contracted deliverables, thereby reducing ecosystem fragility over a 12-month horizon.

## 3-Stage Program Framework

```
[ Stage 1: Discovery & Mapping ] ──> [ Stage 2: Validation & Planning ] ──> [ Stage 3: Execution & De-risking ]
        (Months 1 – 3)                        (Months 4 – 6)                          (Months 7 – 12)
  • Define Frontier Domain              • Architect Legal Container             • Convert BD Pipeline to Commitments
  • Verify Underfunding                 • Build 3–5 Credibility Assets          • Run External Client Pilots
  • Audit & Consolidate Revenue         • Define ICPs & Sales Collateral        • Harden Operational Rhythms
```

1. **Stage 1: Discovery, Research & Mapping (Months 1–3)**: Defines the team's "frontier domain," confirms it is underfunded and underserved by incumbents, verifies research can be translated into contractable deliverables, and audits/consolidates existing revenue.
2. **Stage 2: Validation & Planning (Months 4–6)**: Architecting the legal and financial container (checking bylaws for earned revenue authorization, managing negative margin runway), building 3 to 5 "credibility assets" (publications, workshops, releases), defining Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs), and assembling BD collateral (one-pagers, statement of work templates, decks).
3. **Stage 3: Execution & De-risking (Months 7–12)**: Signing commercial commitments, running pilots, shipping client-contracted deliverables, and hardening internal operating rhythms to achieve sustainable funding diversification.

## Renaissance Philanthropy Workshop Partnership

Co-designed with **Renaissance Philanthropy** (the team behind the FRC Launchpad / ARIA UK), participants complete three intensive workshops:
- **Workshop 1: Theory of Impact**: Clarifying each team's north-star technical vision to align with target customer needs.
- **Workshop 2: Customer Prospecting (FRC Style)**: Identifying ideal customer profiles, prospecting channels, and assembling a commercial sales pipeline.
- **Workshop 3: Crafting a One-Liner & Blurb**: Developing compelling outreach collateral tailored for technical and commercial buyers.
