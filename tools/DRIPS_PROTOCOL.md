## 1. Primary Function & Capabilities
Drips enables projects to receive continuous streaming funds and automatically split incoming value down their open-source dependency tree.

## 2. Role in dOSPO / OMF / ORF Suite
- **ORF Application Layer**: Provides the primary distribution rail for dependency-directed revenue sharing.
- **Multi-Chain Portability**: Operates across EVM networks and Filecoin, demonstrating portable dependency settlement without requiring protocol-level bytecode attribution.
