# Start here

The lab is three specifications that can be used together or alone. Web3 treasuries are the primary audience. The same questions apply to a Web2 foundation, a company, or a public funder.

The series position, including the note on the OMF PDF, is in [whitepapers/README.md](whitepapers/README.md). Terms are in [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md). Nothing here marks a [hard gate](GLOSSARY.md#hard-gates) passed.

## If you hold a treasury

Read [QUICKSTART_FOR_TREASURIES.md](QUICKSTART_FOR_TREASURIES.md), then the five questions in [orf/5_QUESTION_ASSESSMENT.md](orf/5_QUESTION_ASSESSMENT.md).

## If you maintain a project

Read [docs/MAINTAINER_AUTONOMY_GUIDE.md](docs/MAINTAINER_AUTONOMY_GUIDE.md), then [omf/PROGRAM_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md](omf/PROGRAM_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md).
