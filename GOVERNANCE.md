# Open Source Frontiers Lab Governance

> **Project Charter & Governance Model**  
> *LF Decentralized Trust · Open Source Frontiers Lab*

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Open Source Frontiers Lab** operates as an open technical initiative hosted within **LF Decentralized Trust**. Its mission is to research, design, evaluate, and publish normative guidance, economic models, governance frameworks, and reference implementations for open-source sustainability in Web3 and decentralized ecosystems.

---

## 2. Governance Principles

The project adheres to four core governance principles:

1. **Vendor Neutrality**: No single corporate entity, foundation, or commercial vendor holds exclusive authority over specifications, models, or evaluations.
2. **Evidence-Based Validation**: Specifications and maturity classifications rely on auditable empirical evidence, peer review, and transparent evaluation rubrics.
3. **Operator Replaceability**: Maintainer roles and administrative functions are time-limited, transparent, and community-replaceable.
4. **Open Technical Contributions**: All decisions, specifications, code, and evaluators are developed in the open under Apache-2.0 and CC-BY-4.0 licenses.

---

## 3. Maintainer Roles & Decision Making

- **Lead Architect / Maintainer**: Responsible for overall architectural coherence, specification review, and release tagging.
- **Maintainers**: Community members with commit authority responsible for reviewing PRs, maintaining evaluators, and auditing ecosystem profiles.
- **Decision Process**: Technical decisions are made through consensus on GitHub Pull Requests. Substantive specification changes require review from at least one maintainer prior to merge.

---

## 4. DCO & Licensing Compliance

All contributions strictly enforce the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)** via `git commit -s`. Code is licensed under Apache-2.0 and documentation under CC-BY-4.0.
