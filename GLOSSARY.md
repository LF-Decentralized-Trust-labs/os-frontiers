# Framework Glossary & Terminology

> **LF Decentralized Trust · Open Source Frontiers Lab**

---

## Acronyms & Core Definitions

- **dOSPO (Decentralized Open Source Program Office)**: The **WHO / Governance Authority** layer defining bounded mandate, funding principles, neutrality rules, transparency standards, and operator replaceability.
- **OMF (Open Maintenance Framework)**: The **HOW SPEND / Deployment** layer standardizing maintainer retainers, dependency audits, contributor ladders, and stewardship programs.
- **ORF (Open Replenishment Framework)**: The **HOW COLLECT / Inflow** layer detailing 20+ non-inflationary collection mechanisms (enterprise SLAs, badging, IPS endowments, stake pool margins).
- **POSM (Paid Open Source Model)**: An operational maintainer-retainer precursor implemented within the Cardano ecosystem under Intersect MBO governance.
- **IPS (Investment Policy Statement)**: A governed investment framework defining risk limits, liquidity sleeves, and yield parameters for treasury reserve management.
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)**: Enterprise maintenance and response agreements providing commercial funding for core open-source infrastructure.
- **QUAID**: An open-source repository health and security scanner. OSF provides an experimental heuristic adapter (`evaluator/cli/quaid_adapter.py`).
- **RACI Matrix**: Responsibility assignment matrix defining who is **Responsible**, **Accountable**, **Consulted**, and **Informed** across framework operations.
- **DCO (Developer Certificate of Origin)**: A lightweight mechanism for contributors to certify that they wrote or have the right to submit code (`git commit -s`).
- **Net Replenishment Ratio**: The ratio of non-inflationary earned revenue (SLAs, certs, badges, pool margins, protocol fee splits) divided by baseline maintenance operating costs. A ratio $\ge 1.0$ indicates a self-sustaining economic loop.
