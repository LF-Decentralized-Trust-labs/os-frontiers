# Quickstart for treasuries

A treasury can use this without adopting every instrument. Web3 treasuries are the primary case. A Web2 foundation, a company, or a public funder can use the same five questions. No [hard gate](GLOSSARY.md#hard-gates) is marked passed. Do not treat a point score as a pass.

## Five questions

Score them in [orf/5_QUESTION_ASSESSMENT.md](orf/5_QUESTION_ASSESSMENT.md). The questions are:

1. **Ratio.** What share of the maintenance cost comes back as non-inflationary income, and what fills the gap?
2. **Anchors.** Does each collection instrument attach to something the payer would not give up, rather than to a fee on reading the code?
3. **Bundles.** Is every optional payment tied to a benefit the payer wants on its own?
4. **Mandate.** Is each collection instrument chartered, time-bounded, and publicly reported?
5. **Runway.** Can baseline maintenance continue through a multi-year downturn without an emergency draw on reserves?

## Three starter programs

These are spending programs ([OMF](GLOSSARY.md#omf)), not revenue. Specifications: [omf/PROGRAM_PORTFOLIO.md](omf/PROGRAM_PORTFOLIO.md). Charter them before funding them: [omf/PROGRAM_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md](omf/PROGRAM_CHARTER_TEMPLATE.md).

1. **Maintainer retainers.** Predictable pay for people who already maintain the code the treasury depends on.
2. **Contributor pathways.** A written ladder so the retainer is not a single person.
3. **Resilience work.** A dependency audit and a small security budget for the same code, reported on a schedule.

[dOSPO](GLOSSARY.md#dospo) sets the charter and the cap. It does not hold the treasury. Read [dospo/NON_POWERS.md](dospo/NON_POWERS.md) before the first transfer.

## One replenishment instrument

Start with one instrument, not a portfolio. The catalog's first commercial instrument that does not require a support contract is **Open Infrastructure Assurance** (Family B, Instrument B.1) in [orf/INSTRUMENT_CATALOG.md](orf/INSTRUMENT_CATALOG.md).

Do not sell a maintenance SLA until the service-capacity test in [orf/GOVERNANCE_RULES.md](orf/GOVERNANCE_RULES.md) is met. Assurance is risk information. An SLA is a promise. The test is what separates them.

This choice is a starting point in the specification. It is not a claim that any treasury has passed a gate with it.

## Quarterly reporting

Publish one quarterly note. Use [omf/QUARTERLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md](omf/QUARTERLY_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) for the spending side. For anything collected, the ORF note in [orf/GOVERNANCE_RULES.md](orf/GOVERNANCE_RULES.md) (section 8) asks for gross receipts by family, cost to collect, net transferred, payer concentration, mechanism concentration, stressed runway, and the status of any liabilities.
