## Executive Summary
This proposal establishes a **Decentralized Open Source Program Office (dOSPO)** governance mandate and launches a **Tier 1 Open Replenishment Framework (ORF)** pilot for `[Ecosystem Name]`.

Currently, our ecosystem deploys capital to open-source builders through grants and retroactive funding, but lacks a mechanism to collect value back into the treasury. This proposal establishes a time-limited 12-month pilot to:
1. Form a community-mandated **dOSPO Committee** to manage core maintenance priorities.
2. Launch an **Enterprise Maintenance SLA Program** to generate earned revenue from corporate adopters.
3. Establish a **Capital Layer Investment Policy Statement (IPS)** to convert static reserves into yield.

---

## 1. The Governance Mandate (dOSPO)
The dOSPO Committee will operate under a time-limited 12-month charter with the following bounded powers:
- **Legitimacy**: Mandate authorized directly by token-holder / DRep vote.
- **Neutrality**: No committee member may hold commercial conflicts of interest or purchase technical roadmap authority.
- **Replaceability**: Governance retains full authority to replace committee members or terminate the mandate upon a 51% vote.

---

## 2. Maintenance Deployment (OMF)
The dOSPO will administer **Open Maintenance Framework (OMF)** programs:
- Core Maintainer Retainers for top repositories: `[List key repos]`
- Tooling Stewardship and dependency security auditing.
- *Autonomy Safeguard*: Maintainers retain 100% technical control over project roadmaps.

---

## 3. Value Replenishment Mechanics (ORF Tier 1)
The pilot will activate three initial non-inflationary collection rails:
1. **Enterprise Maintenance SLAs**: Offer enterprise patch windows, compatibility guidance, and SLA response targets for commercial adopters.
2. **Voluntary Application Badging**: Applications routing a 1% fee share receive official "Sustains the Commons" registry certification.
3. **Treasury Endowment IPS**: Authorize a 20% stablecoin reserve sleeve into governed liquid staking yield.
