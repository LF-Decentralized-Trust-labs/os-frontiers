# Open Source Frontiers — Executive One-Pager

> **A 3-Piece Framework Suite for Self-Sustaining Open Source Ecosystems (dOSPO · OMF · ORF)**

---

## The Executive Problem

Open-source software powers global digital infrastructure, yet token-funded ecosystems face a structural flaw: **they spend capital on sustainability without collecting value back into the treasury**. 

Relying indefinitely on token inflation or finite reserves creates a ticking clock. When bear markets arrive, maintenance budgets collapse, core maintainers leave, and infrastructure security degrades.

---

## The Solution: A 3-Piece Recipe (Distinct Functional Mandates)

```
                     COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE
                  Mandate · Budget · Rules
                            |
                            v
                         dOSPO
                Coordinate · Draft · Report
                      /             \
                     v               v
                  OMF                 ORF
               DEPLOY              REPLENISH
           maintenance capital   economic inflows
                     \               /
                      \             /
                       v           v
                  GOVERNED TREASURY
                            |
                            v
                   INDEPENDENT AUDIT
```

1. **dOSPO (Governance Policy — *WHO*)**: Bounded, community-mandated policy & coordination body holding the governance triangle (**legitimacy, neutrality, execution**). Holds zero direct discretionary treasury custody and is fully replaceable by community vote.
2. **OMF (Maintenance Deployment — *HOW SPEND*)**: Portfolio of operational programs (Maintainer Retainers, Contributor Pathways, Tooling Stewardship) protecting maintainer autonomy.
3. **ORF (Value Replenishment — *HOW COLLECT*)**: Portfolio of collection instruments matched to where value is realized across Protocol, Application, Enterprise, Capital, and Delegation layers.

---

## Core Governing Principles

- **Value Alignment**: Collect where value is realized (Protocol fees, App revenue, Enterprise SLAs, Treasury yield).
- **Fork Resistance**: Attach capture to non-copyable assets (canonical state, liquidity, registry badges, SLAs).
- **Benefit Bundling**: Sold, not taxed. Enterprise patch windows, SLAs, and registry badges offered while security intake remains free and universal.
- **Mandate Governance**: Every spending program and collection rail requires a traceable mandate, time-limited charter, and independent audit review.

---

## Why Ecosystems Adopt the Suite

- **For DAOs & Governance**: Replaces ad-hoc grant spending with a self-sustaining financial feedback loop.
- **For Maintainers**: Guarantees predictable recurring retainers without sacrificing technical roadmap control.
- **For Enterprise Sponsors**: Provides a compliant commercial interface to purchase maintenance SLAs, patch commitments, and supply-chain assurances.
