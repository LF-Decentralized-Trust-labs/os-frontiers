## Executive Summary for Enterprise Buyers

Your company relies on open-source infrastructure and decentralized protocols for critical business operations. However, consuming open-source software without an enterprise support structure exposes your organization to:
- **Unpredictable Patching**: Critical security vulnerabilities left un-patched in older release lines.
- **Breaking API Changes**: Sudden protocol upgrades breaking internal enterprise integrations.
- **Supply-Chain Attacks**: Malicious dependency padding or compromised package registries.

The **Open Replenishment Framework (ORF) Enterprise Maintenance SLA** converts open-source consumption from a high-risk gamble into a professional, enterprise-grade service contract.

## Procurement & Legal Compliance

- **Compliant Commercial Contracts**: Delivered as a standard B2B service contract with defined Scope of Work (SOW), SLA targets, and liability caps.
- **Tax & Accounting Treatment**: Deductible as standard software maintenance & IT support expense (not a donation or charitable grant).
- **Neutral Legal Operator**: Contracted through an authorized foundation entity (such as Linux Foundation LFX, PCF, or dOSPO operator).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does purchasing an SLA give us control over technical project roadmaps?**  
*A: No. Payment purchases enterprise support services and patch windows; it never purchases technical voting rights or roadmap authority over open-source projects.*

**Q: How are maintainers compensated?**  
*A: A designated share of SLA contract revenue is routed directly to maintainer retainer pools via transparent dOSPO program charters.*
